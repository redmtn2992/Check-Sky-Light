## Multi-Modal Forensic Screening Protocols

Used by the engine to evaluate raw media files, YouTube URLs, and social media submissions:

1. **Error Level Analysis (ELA)**: Re-compression difference maps detecting composite tampering or spliced image patches.
2. **Sensor Noise Floor & CFA Consistency**: Color Filter Array (CFA) demosaicing evaluation and camera CMOS noise profiles vs synthetic render interpolation.
3. **Parallax Geometry Solver**: Calculates camera platform velocity (e.g. 150–200 knot MQ-9 Reaper cruise) against ground terrain to decouple apparent high velocity from true target airspeed.
4. **Thermal Signature Characterization**:
   * *Avian Biology*: Low thermal delta (~40°C), soft thermal bloom masking wing-beat cadence.
   * *Tactical Flares (LUU-2)*: Extreme thermal saturation (>2,000°C), smoke trails, vertical parachute descent (~8 ft/s).
   * *Combustion Exhaust*: Jet turbine / rocket plumes with visible Rayleigh scattering and thermal ionization.
   * *Metric Candidate*: Absence of thermal plume, absence of acoustic shockwaves, and non-inertial vector changes.
