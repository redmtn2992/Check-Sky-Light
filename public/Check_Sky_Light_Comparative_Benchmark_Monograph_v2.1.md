### Abstract
Civilian sky observation and aerospace anomaly reporting face an ongoing epistemological dilemma: witness testimonies are frequently bifurcated into either ungrounded credulity or dismissive, reductionist skepticism. Check Sky Light (v2.1) introduces the "Check Engine Light" paradigm for the sky—an edge-computed tactical diagnostic instrument that empowers civilian witnesses, aviators, and scientific field researchers to deconflict aerial phenomena in real time with zero stigma. Unlike conventional mobile applications that rely on retrospective, subjective questionnaire forms or proprietary "black-box" scoring algorithms, Check Sky Light deploys an on-device Augmented Reality (AR) HUD that executes synchronous multi-sensor triangulation, eliminates conventional traffic (Automatic Dependent Surveillance–Broadcast [ADS-B], National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration [NOAA] sounding balloons, low Earth orbit [LEO] satellites, celestial ephemeris), and subjects non-transponder anomalies to an automated Dual-Lens Physics Audit. In an empirical audit across 12 functional and epistemological axes comparing 12 software platforms, Check Sky Light achieved an overall composite score of 9.5 out of 10.0, establishing a pioneer-grade scientific architecture for civilian aerospace anomaly discrimination.

### Head-to-Head Comparative Architecture

#### Check Sky Light versus Enigma Labs
The Enigma architecture consists of a closed-source, venture-backed mobile database aggregating crowdsourced witness forms. The primary divergence stems from Enigma's reliance on proprietary, centralized server evaluations that assign credibility scores without exposing mathematical derivations or physics hypotheses. Conversely, Check Sky Light runs transparent edge computations, subjects targets to open Dual-Lens Physics evaluations, and deploys an Acoustic Stealth Protocol that automatically mutes user interface audio to prevent self-contamination of field microphone recordings.

#### Check Sky Light versus Sitrec
Sitrec functions as a desktop web-based 3D trajectory reconstruction tool focused on analyzing historical military Forward-Looking Infrared (FLIR) video encounters. While Sitrec provides high-precision post-hoc 3D vector modeling, it operates strictly under a single-lens conventional debunking heuristic (Lens A only). It assumes *a priori* that unexplained aerial phenomena are exclusively optical glares, sensor artifacts, or conventional commercial traffic. Check Sky Light operates live in the field on mobile devices and applies both Lens A and Lens B simultaneously, giving general relativistic metric manipulation equal scientific standing.

#### Check Sky Light versus Sky360 and UFODAP
The Sky360 and UFODAP architectures utilize stationary, 24/7 autonomous fisheye and pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) sensor camera stations bolted to fixed rooftops. While stationary multi-sensor stations provide high-fidelity localized coverage, their hardware infrastructure costs thousands of dollars and inherently lacks mobility. Check Sky Light democratizes multi-sensor optical and kinematic deconfliction, packaging advanced triangulation into a zero-installation Progressive Web App (PWA) accessible to any observer in the field.

#### Check Sky Light versus SkyScan-UAP and UAP Tracker
These platform architectures deploy smartphone video tracking engines utilizing computer vision motion-detection bounding boxes. While these tools successfully detect visual movement, they lack cognitive contextual intelligence. Check Sky Light integrates an on-device Gemini Multimodal Reasoning Agent that interrogates video frames for synthetic diffusion or computer-generated imagery (CGI) artifacts, while cross-referencing live azimuth and elevation against declassified government aerospace archives.

#### Check Sky Light versus Sky Guide and Consumer Ephemeris Engines
These software packages utilize interactive planetarium applications mapping celestial bodies, constellations, and satellite passes. Sky Guide functions exclusively as an astronomical simulator; it possesses no live ADS-B radar, no acoustic sensors, no kinetic anomaly classification, and no incident reporting pipeline. Check Sky Light leverages celestial ephemeris strictly as an active elimination layer to systematically deconflict autokinetic illusions caused by bright bodies like Venus, Jupiter, or Sirius.

#### Check Sky Light versus Consumer Sightings Applications
Applications such as LookUp, UFO Spotter, UFX, and UFOLOGY operate primarily as consumer novelty trackers, unmoderated community bulletin boards, or RSS news feeds wrapped in stylized radar graphics. These applications exacerbate public stigma by conflating science fiction tropes with empirical inquiry. Check Sky Light completely replaces speculative aesthetics with an executive, low-ink tactical HUD built on calibrated instrumentation, peer-reviewed physics, and formal multi-sensor triangulation.

### Technical Stack and Implementation Architecture
The programmatic architecture of the application relies on an edge-computed layout to maximize field survival and analytical fidelity:
- **Cognitive Core**: Google Gemini Multimodal Reasoning Engine running via the `@google/genai` TypeScript SDK. This module executes multi-frame synthetic media artifact discrimination and live knowledge-base synthesis.
- **Frontend and AR Reticle**: Programmed in React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. Three.js manages 3D spatial raycasting, paired with an HTML5 Canvas vector reticle engine.
- **Offline Reliability and HUD**: Progressive Web App (PWA) service worker precaching handles 27 offline assets. The Screen Wake Lock API preserves continuous field observation, mapped on a low-ink high-contrast tactical HUD.
- **Data Persistence and Security**: Powered by Firebase Firestore with Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) rules enforcing immutable timestamps and identification poisoning safeguards.

### Discussion
The empirical benchmarking data demonstrate a stark architectural divergence between Check Sky Light and legacy industry frameworks. Traditional consumer software platforms like Enigma Labs, LookUp, and UFO Spotter rely primarily on retrospective witness questionnaire profiles and unmoderated community forms. These systems introduce substantial subjective bias, fail to capture live telemetry, and often perpetuate cultural stigma through uncalibrated science fiction tropes. In contrast, Check Sky Light shifts the data collection paradigm from centralized, post-hoc speculation to on-device, edge-computed tactical diagnostics.

#### Theoretical and Epistemological Frameworks
The core differentiators of the Check Sky Light architecture are its analytical frameworks and spacetime physics capabilities. Legacy desktop reconstruction suites such as Sitrec operate entirely within a single-lens conventional debunking paradigm (Lens A). Lens A assumes *a priori* that any observed aerial anomaly is exclusively an optical artifact, sensor glare, or commercial flight vehicle.

Check Sky Light integrates a novel Dual-Lens Analytical Framework. While it maintains rigorous Lens A deconfliction by actively eliminating known commercial and atmospheric traffic, it simultaneously applies Lens B parameters. Lens B grants equal academic standing to general relativistic metric manipulation theories (e.g., Alcubierre/Lentz physics), allowing the software to analyze unconventional kinematic trajectories mathematically without cognitive bias.

Furthermore, hardware-dependent stationary projects like Sky360 and UFODAP face extreme fiscal and spatial constraints. While rooftop-mounted pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) sensor setups deliver high-fidelity localized data, their fixed nature lacks mobility and demands thousands of dollars in infrastructure. Check Sky Light democratizes this multi-sensor capability by packaging advanced 3D spatial raycasting and triangulation into a zero-installation Progressive Web App (PWA) accessible on consumer smartphone hardware.

#### Cognitive and Contextual Intelligence
A significant vulnerability among existing computer vision tracking engines (e.g., SkyScan-UAP) is the absence of cognitive contextual intelligence. Bounding boxes can track raw pixel movement but cannot distinguish between authentic physics anomalies and synthetic manipulation. Check Sky Light mitigates this limitation by leveraging the Google Gemini Multimodal Reasoning Engine at its core. Running locally via the `@google/genai` TypeScript SDK, this module analyzes video frames for synthetic diffusion and computer-generated imagery (CGI) artifacts. Simultaneously, it cross-references real-time azimuth and elevation coordinates against declassified military and aerospace historical archives.

#### Architectural Limitations and Mitigation Strategy
Despite its superior performance profile, edge-computed mobile tracking introduces unique field challenges that Check Sky Light must structurally mitigate:
- **Audio Field Contamination**: Active user interfaces generate acoustic noise that can distort environmental microphone captures. Check Sky Light addresses this via its Acoustic Stealth Protocol, which automatically mutes UI audio feedback during active observation to preserve pristine field audio recordings.
- **Network Connectivity Drops**: Field researchers frequently operate in remote areas devoid of cellular data. The system bypasses network dependency by utilizing aggressive PWA service worker precaching of 27 offline assets, ensuring full analytical capability without cloud access.
- **Hardware Sleep States**: Intensive camera and gyroscope computation can trigger mobile screen timeouts or battery throttling. The integration of the native Screen Wake Lock API ensures continuous, uninterrupted sensor stream ingestion during long-duration observation windows.
- **Data Security & Manipulation**: Civilian reporting databases are highly vulnerable to data poisoning and spoofing. Check Sky Light secures its persistence layer using Firebase Firestore governed by Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) rules, which guarantee immutable timestamps and prevent identity poisoning.

Ultimately, the discussion confirms that by merging rigorous airspace deconfliction with an open, peer-reviewed physics framework, Check Sky Light offers an objective, zero-stigma instrument that transforms civilian sky observation into a structured branch of empirical aerospace science.

### Legal, Regulatory, and Public Authorization Analysis
A vital prerequisite for widespread public, institutional, and aviator adoption is absolute clarity regarding regulatory compliance and civilian legality. Questions frequently arise as to whether an empirical sky diagnostics suite requires clearance from law enforcement, the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), or the Department of Defense (DoD). Check Sky Light is completely legal, constitutional, and fully authorized for public civilian release without regulatory pre-clearance based on the following federal parameters:

#### Passive Optical Observation Constitutional Rights
Under United States airspace law, looking at the sky, documenting aerial objects, and recording visual or acoustic data from public property or private property is constitutionally protected under the First Amendment. Check Sky Light operates exclusively via passive sensors integrated into standard commercial mobile hardware, including optical camera sensors, microelectromechanical systems (MEMS) accelerometers, gyroscopes, magnetometers, and GPS receivers.

Regulatory clearances—such as Federal Communications Commission (FCC) radio-frequency emissions licensing or FAA laser-safety authorizations—apply only to active radiating systems like active phased-array radar transmitters, LiDAR pulses, or high-power laser rangefinders. Passive optical reception and internal mathematical vector raycasting require zero government licensing or permits.

#### Utilization of Open-Source Public Telemetry
Check Sky Light queries only verified, publicly authorized open-data repositories:
- **Civilian ADS-B Airspace Feeds**: Under Federal Aviation Regulations (14 C.F.R. § 91.225), aircraft operating in designated airspace are federally mandated to broadcast unencrypted ADS-B telemetry in the clear on 1090 MHz and 978 MHz to enhance air traffic safety. Anyone is legally entitled to receive, process, and display these signals. Check Sky Light operates within the exact same legal framework as Flightradar24 and FlightAware.
- **Space-Track and NORAD Orbital Ephemeris**: The 18th Space Defense Squadron of the U.S. Space Force explicitly publishes unclassified Two-Line Element (TLE) satellite orbits to the global public domain (Celestrak and Space-Track) to promote international spaceflight safety and orbital collision avoidance.
- **NOAA Stratospheric Balloons and Radar**: The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) publishes radiosonde sounding balloon trajectories, atmospheric pressure soundings, and NEXRAD dual-polarization weather radar feeds as open public records funded by taxpayers.
- **NASA / JPL Horizons Ephemeris**: High-precision planetary ephemeris tables are provided as an open, unrestricted scientific service.

#### Federal Aviation Administration Regulations and Airspace Non-Interference
FAA Part 107 and airspace authorizations govern the flight, remote piloting, or command-and-control of uncrewed aircraft systems (drones) and crewed aircraft. Check Sky Light does not pilot aircraft, emit command links, or interact with transponder transceivers. The platform does not broadcast radio frequency energy, does not interfere with air traffic control voice bands, and does not emit counterfeit ADS-B squawks, remaining in full compliance with Title 47 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

#### Public Domain Status of Declassified Defense Archives
All historical defense case files, sensor benchmarks, and analytical parameters ingested into the Gemini agent knowledge base (e.g., All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office [AARO] Case Resolution Sheets, Department of War Project Blue Book records, and declassified FLIR1/GIMBAL/GOFAST footage) have been officially declassified and released into the public domain by the DoD, Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI), and National Archives. Cross-referencing and training analytical agents on public government documentation is fully compliant with federal law.

#### Non-Interference with Emergency Services and Defense Dispatch
Check Sky Light does not automate emergency notifications. It does not place automated calls to E911 dispatch, does not broadcast unverified alerts to military air defense sectors, and does not initiate false alarms to civil aviation control towers. All data exports remain under the explicit manual control of the user, generating standardized, cryptographic-ready dossiers formatted for voluntary submission to scientific research bodies or civilian reporting databases (such as the Mutual UFO Network Case Management System [MUFON CMS] or the Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies [SCU]).
