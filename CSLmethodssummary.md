## PART 1: Core Analytical Architecture

### 1. The Dual-Lens Analytical Framework
Check Sky Light operates as a rigorous decision-support engine rather than an uncritical classifier. It rejects binary heuristics ("alien vs. mundane hoax") in favor of empirical deconfliction governed by two simultaneous, complementary analytical perspectives:

* **Lens A: Classical Aerospace Baseline (Conventional Mechanics & Aviation Safety)**
  * **Mechanisms**: Evaluates evidence against Newtonian fluid dynamics, atmospheric shear, aerodynamic lifting surfaces, combustion/turbofan plumes, acoustic shockwaves ($N$-waves / sonic booms), and FAA Title 14 CFR § 91.209 anti-collision strobes (1.2 Hz cadence) and position lighting.
  * **Deconfliction Spectrum**: Identifies commercial airliners, military aircraft, low-Earth orbit satellites (Starlink, ISS, OneWeb via NORAD Two-Line Elements), commercial/consumer drones (FAA Part 107), meteorological radiosonde balloons, biological fauna (birds, insects creating optical parallax "rods"), specular reflections, and sensor artifacts (rolling shutter, digital macroblocking, lens flare).

* **Lens B: Theoretical Metric Manipulation & Non-Conventional Physics**
  * **Mechanisms**: Acknowledges that authentic Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) and potential Non-Human Intelligence (NHI) technology may operate via localized spacetime metric engineering (e.g., Alcubierre-type distortion tensors, Lentz positive-energy solitons, or geodesic frame-dragging).
  * **Physical Decoupling Principle**: Recognizes that advanced metric manipulation naturally decouples a craft from the surrounding ambient fluid medium. Under this framework:
    * The **absence of barometric downwash or rotor wash** is not an impossibility—it is the direct theoretical signature of gravitational or field-propulsion decoupling.
    * **Transmedium travel** (vacuum $\rightarrow$ atmosphere $\rightarrow$ ocean) occurs without hydrodynamic drag, cavitation, water displacement, or thermal heating.
    * **Hypersonic acceleration** occurs without acoustic sonic booms ($N$-waves) or atmospheric thermal ionization.
  * **The Five Observables Baseline**: Evaluates sightings against the formal criteria established by ODNI/AARO: instantaneous acceleration, hypersonic velocity without signatures, low observability/cloaking, transmedium travel, and positive lift without aerodynamic surfaces.

* *Lens A Finding*: Formally states: *"Civilian airliner hypothesis disqualified due to non-compliant lighting and acoustic silence; terrestrial candidates are strictly confined to un-transponded military black-budget prototypes or synchronized drone arrays."*

## PART 5: Mathematical Scoring Architecture

CSL computes three normalized composite scores (0 to 100):

### 1. Anomaly Score ($S_{\text{anomaly}}$: 0 to 100)
Quantifies the departure from classical aerospace, meteorological, and celestial baselines:

$$S_{\text{anomaly}} = \sum_{i=1}^{5} w_i \cdot O_i + W_{\text{phys}} \cdot P_{\text{kinematic}} - D_{\text{conv}}$$

Where:
* $O_1 \dots O_5$ are the Five ODNI/AARO Observables (Instantaneous Acceleration, Hypersonic Velocity without signatures, Low Observability, Transmedium Travel, Positive Lift without aerodynamic surfaces), weighted from 0 to 15 points each.
* $P_{\text{kinematic}}$ measures calculated g-force ($a / g$) and angular velocity ($\omega$). Acceleration exceeding human physiological tolerance ($>9g$) or conventional airframe structural limits ($>45g$) drives this component to its maximum ($+25$ points).
* $D_{\text{conv}}$ is the Conventional Deconfliction Penalty (deducts up to $-60$ points if the kinematic path matches ballistic satellite decay, FAA beacon flash rates at 1.2 Hz, commercial cruise speed/altitude, or astronomical coordinates).

### 2. Confidence Score ($S_{\text{confidence}}$: 0 to 100)
Reflects the epistemic reliability and signal-to-noise ratio of the submitted data:

$$S_{\text{confidence}} = 0.35 \cdot M_{\text{sensor}} + 0.30 \cdot C_{\text{triangulation}} + 0.20 \cdot Q_{\text{media}} + 0.15 \cdot E_{\text{metadata}}$$

* $M_{\text{sensor}}$: Multi-sensor corroboration (Optical + FLIR + Radar primary returns = 100; single smartphone optical = 30).
* $C_{\text{triangulation}}$: Rigor of spatial intersection based on Miss Distance ($d_{\text{miss}} < 50\text{ m} \implies 100$; uncorroborated single line-of-sight = 15).
* $Q_{\text{media}}$: Visual clarity, resolution, dynamic range, and absence of compression degradation.
* $E_{\text{metadata}}$: Authenticity of EXIF/metadata, GPS lock precision, and NTP synchronized timestamps.

### 3. Credibility & Forensic Index (0 to 100)
Measures the resistance of the evidence to digital forgery, CGI tampering, or misinterpretation:
* **Digital Tampering Index (DTI)**: Evaluates compression artifacts, edge continuity, chromatic aberration consistency, and noise floor homogeneity across the image plane.
* **Witness Credibility Factor**: Evaluates named on-the-record eyewitnesses, commercial/military pilot credentials, lack of commercial monetization, and unscripted acoustic situational shock.
