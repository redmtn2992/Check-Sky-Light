1. Executive Summary & Purpose ↑ Back to TOC

Check Sky Light is an open, empirical civilian sky diagnostics and aerospace deconfliction platform. It bridges the divide between naive skepticism and uncritical credulity by providing citizen scientists, field researchers, and observers with a calibrated, real-time sensor instrument in their pocket.

Traditional sky observation apps either function as passive crowdsourced reporting forms or closed proprietary black boxes. Check Sky Light transforms a standard modern smartphone into an active aerospace diagnostic station by fusing:

  • Device Sensor Fusion: Hardware-calibrated IMU orientation (pitch, azimuth, roll, angular velocity), high-frame-rate optical capture, and acoustic microphone preservation.
  • Multi-Domain Airspace Telemetry: Real-time cross-referencing against live ADS-B civilian/military transponders, NOAA weather balloon soundings, and NORAD Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite trajectories.
  • The Parallax Panel (Tri-Model AI Architecture): Collaborative cross-examination between three independent frontier AI engines (Gemini, Claude, and Luna) enforcing strict empirical falsifiability.
  • The Dual-Lens Analytical Framework: Simultaneous evaluation under Lens A (Classical Fluid Dynamics & Aerospace Baseline) and Lens B (Theoretical Spacetime Metric Manipulation).

2. The Four Operational Pillars ↑ Back to TOC

Check Sky Light is architected around four distinct operational modules, accessible via the bottom tactical navigation dock:

🎯 1. TARGET (Optical AR Tracking HUD)

  • Real-Time Reticle HUD: Overlays high-contrast, subpixel-stabilized azimuth (0°–360°), elevation pitch (-90° to +90°), roll, and angular tracking rates (°/sec) directly onto the live camera stream.
  • Telephoto & Optical Filtration: Digital/optical crop-zoom controls (1x, 2x, 3x, 5x) combined with Day-HDR, Night-Starlight, and High-Contrast optical filters for acquiring high-altitude targets.
  • Acoustic Silence Tenet: Operates completely silent by default (synthesizers and shutter sounds muted) so the microphone records clean, uncontaminated ambient acoustic signatures.

📡 2. RADAR (Live Airspace Deconfliction)

  • ADS-B Transponder Integration: Live tracking of commercial airliners, private aviation, and unblocked transponders within the observer's visual radius.
  • NOAA Weather Balloon Tracking: Live telemetry from national weather service radiosonde launches (frequency, ascent rate, altitude MSL).
  • LEO Satellite Ephemeris: Orbital trajectory vectors for Starlink trains, the International Space Station (ISS), and prominent orbital debris.
  • Deconfliction-First Principle: Automatically weeds out known conventional flights before any anomaly claim is considered.

🔬 3. ANALYZE (Multi-Lens Forensic AI Lab)

  • Digital Media & CGI Forensics: Screens uploaded images and video frames for synthetic diffusion noise, edge matting splines, 3D camera tracking drift, and post-production keyframing.
  • Dual-Lens Analytical Engine: Tests footage against both Lens A (FAA anti-collision strobes, aerodynamic surfaces, Newtonian drag) and Lens B (Metric manipulation, vacuum shearing, absence of sonic booms).
  • Tri-Model Consensus: Multi-agent validation across Gemini, Claude, and Luna to eliminate single-model hallucinations.

📋 4. REPORT (Diagnostic Dossiers & Archival)

  • Automotive Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTC): Assigns standardized fault codes (e.g., DTC P0312, DTC P0420) to communicate encounter severity and category instantly.
  • Multi-Observer Triangulation: Calculates true 3D geometric altitude (ft MSL) and slant range (mi) using simultaneous two-point witness sightline intersections.
  • Audit-Ready Export: Generates tamper-evident, publication-grade PDF dossiers complete with sensor provenance stamps ready for submission to academic and governmental repositories (AARO, SUAPS, SCU).

Detailed Application Critiques

  • Check Sky Light (v3.6) — 9.5/10: Total convergence. Fuses live AR tracking, immediate multi-source airspace deconfliction, multi-model AI forensic evaluation, and formal PDF dossiers into an accessible, zero-install mobile application. (Limitation: Handheld tool; does not provide 24/7 all-sky stationary camera surveillance).
  • Enigma Labs — 5.5/10: High social polish and strong funding, but functions as a closed "black box". Scoring relies on opaque proprietary algorithms without peer review. Lacks a live optical AR telemetry HUD, multi-model AI consensus, and downloadable raw mathematical triangulation dossiers.
  • Sky360 Network — 6.6/10: Exceptional open-source citizen science community with continuous 24/7 all-sky coverage. However, the high financial and technical barrier to entry (specialized rooftop camera rigs) makes it inaccessible for everyday mobile field use.
  • UFODAP — 5.5/10: Outstanding multi-spectral scientific hardware (optical, RF, magnetic), but dated software interface, zero mobile utility, and costs thousands of dollars.
  • Skywatcher.ai — 6.8/10: Leading autonomous CV tracking algorithms, but tailored for enterprise/research installations rather than spontaneous civilian field encounters.
  • MUFON CMS Mobile — 2.7/10: Form-based questionnaire backlog. No live camera sensor integration, no live ADS-B deconfliction, and investigations take months to process manually.

5. The Evolving 5-Tier Empirical Knowledge Base ↑ Back to TOC

To maintain scientific rigor and prevent contamination from internet folklore, Check Sky Light anchors its diagnostics against five authoritative scientific tiers:

  • Tier I: Official Government & Defense Declassification: AARO (aaro.mil), NASA UAP Independent Study, CNES/GEIPAN (France), ODNI threat assessments.
  • Tier II: Academic & Institutional Scientific Bodies: Society for UAP Studies (SUAPS) / Limina Journal, Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies (SCU), The Galileo Project (Harvard), AIAA UAPIOC.
  • Tier III: Autonomous Sky Observation Networks: Skywatcher.ai, Sky360 Network, UFODAP, Project Hessdalen (Norway, recording since 1983).
  • Tier IV: Spaceflight, Telemetry & Ephemeris Deconfliction: Space-Track / Celestrak (NORAD 18th Space Defense Squadron), ADS-B Exchange, NOAA NEXRAD dual-pol radar.
  • Tier V: Theoretical Metric & Decoupled Physics: Alcubierre (1994) warp metric, Lentz (2021) positive-energy solitons, Bobrick & Martire (2021), boundary-layer vacuum shearing models.
