## 2. Detailed Knowledge Base Descriptions

### Tier I: Official Government, Defense & Aerospace Declassifications

* **`KB-T1-01` • DoD All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO)**
  * *Description*: The official Department of Defense organization tasked with standardizing UAP collection, investigating encounters, and publishing unclassified findings. CSL references AARO reporting standards, Case Resolution Sheets (used as Negative Controls, e.g., Case PR-016 Avian Fauna in Europe), and Unresolved Historical Trends (used as Positive Controls, e.g., Case PR-013 Europe 2022).
* **`KB-T1-02` • PURSUE / US Department of War (`war.gov/ufo`)**
  * *Description*: Official unsealed defense initiatives providing multi-sensor intelligence files (such as FBI-UAP-PR003 "Orbs Over the Pond"). Provides positive control baselines for stationary water hover with zero downwash, positive lift, and non-inertial coordinated departures.
* **`KB-T1-03` • NASA UAP Independent Study Team**
  * *Description*: An independent panel of sixteen leading scientists establishing an unclassified roadmap for applying rigorous data collection, machine learning, and calibrated atmospheric sensors to aerial anomaly investigations.
* **`KB-T1-04` • CNES / GEIPAN (French Space Agency)**
  * *Description*: The longest-running state-sponsored scientific UAP investigation unit in the world (over 40 years). Provides a rigorously cataloged database categorized from Type A (fully resolved) to Type D (unresolved despite high-grade sensor and witness data).
* **`KB-T1-05` • ODNI (Office of the Director of National Intelligence)**
  * *Description*: Annual and preliminary threat assessments produced by the US Intelligence Community. Formally established the **Five Observables** benchmark used by CSL to score non-conventional flight mechanics.
* **`KB-T1-06` • DoD / US Navy Sensor Telemetry (DVIDS)**
  * *Description*: The Defense Visual Information Distribution Service hosting authentic, uncompressed military sensor recordings (ATFLIR MWIR, electro-optical camera footage, and head-up display flight logs).

### Tier II: Academic, Institutional & Aviator Scientific Bodies

* **`KB-T2-01` • Americans for Safe Aerospace (ASA)**
  * *Description*: Founded by former US Navy F/A-18 tactical aviator Lt. Ryan Graves. Focuses on aviation safety, reducing reporting stigma for commercial and military aircrews, and gathering empirical flight-deck data. Provides critical baseline heuristics on what trained pilots see and rule out at cruising altitudes (FL200–FL450).
* **`KB-T2-02` • Merged Podcast (`@safeaerospace`)**
  * *Description*: Technical interview series hosted by Ryan Graves featuring commercial airline captains (United, Delta, American), military fighter pilots, TRACON controllers, and radar engineers. Informs CSL's morphological library (Cube-in-Sphere, Racetrack loops, and Equilateral Black Triangles) and flight-deck sensor cross-checking.
* **`KB-T2-03` • AIAA UAP Integration & Outreach Committee (UAPIOC)**
  * *Description*: Technical committee of the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics. Sets aviation hardware measurement standards, aerodynamic boundary constraints, and pilot reporting protocols.
* **`KB-T2-04` • Scientific Coalition for UAP Studies (SCU)**
  * *Description*: Peer-reviewed scientific collective applying classical physics and Newtonian mechanics to radar and sensor datasets. SCU's peer-reviewed papers on the 2004 Nimitz radar tracks and 2013 Aguadilla transmedium infrared video provide CSL's kinematic g-force formulas.
* **`KB-T2-05` • Society for UAP Studies (SUAPS) & Limina Journal**
  * *Description*: Academic organization publishing the peer-reviewed *Limina: The Journal of UAP Studies*, integrating physical science, philosophy of science, and sensor signal processing.
* **`KB-T2-06` • The Galileo Project (Harvard University / CfA)**
  * *Description*: Led by Prof. Avi Loeb at the Harvard-Smithsonian Center for Astrophysics. Designs and deploys multi-spectral automated observatories (infrared, optical, acoustic, radio) to detect non-human technological signatures without reliance on anecdotal accounts.
* **`KB-T2-07` • The Sol Foundation**
  * *Description*: Academic think tank dedicated to scientific research, public policy, and institutional governance surrounding unexplained aerial and undersea phenomena.

### Tier IV: Real-Time Telemetry, Orbital Ephemeris & Atmospheric Radar

* **`KB-T4-01` • Celestrak / Space-Track (NORAD / 18th Space Defense Squadron)**
  * *Description*: Authoritative orbital Two-Line Element (TLE) ephemeris dataset. Used to propagate real-time coordinates of low-Earth orbit satellites (Starlink trains, OneWeb, ISS, space debris), preventing satellite passes from being misclassified as anomalous formations.
* **`KB-T4-02` • ADS-B Exchange**
  * *Description*: Unfiltered, non-aggregated worldwide flight tracking feed that does not censor military, government, or private aircraft transponders. Critical for ground-truth deconfliction against commercial and military airframes.
* **`KB-T4-03` • OpenSky Network**
  * *Description*: Non-profit academic repository providing open access to historical ADS-B and Mode-S transponder records for post-incident flight path correlation.
* **`KB-T4-04` • Sondehub / APRS Tracker**
  * *Description*: Global live tracking service for meteorological radiosondes and high-altitude research balloons. Computes real-time ascent rates, burst predictions, and wind-drift profiles to eliminate balloon false positives.
* **`KB-T4-05` • NOAA NEXRAD Dual-Polarization Weather Radar**
  * *Description*: National network of high-resolution Doppler radars. Dual-polarization variables ($Z_{\text{DR}}$, $\rho_{\text{HV}}$) are referenced to deconflict biological clutter (bird/bat roost rings, insect swarms), anomalous atmospheric ducting, and chaff.
* **`KB-T4-06` • NASA JPL Horizons System**
