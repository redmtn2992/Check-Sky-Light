## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

**Check Sky Light** is an open, empirical civilian sky diagnostics and aerospace deconfliction platform. It bridges the divide between naive skepticism and uncritical credulity by providing citizen scientists, field researchers, and observers with a calibrated, real-time sensor instrument in their pocket.

Traditional sky observation apps either function as passive crowdsourced reporting forms or closed proprietary black boxes. Check Sky Light transforms a standard modern smartphone into an active aerospace diagnostic station by fusing:
1. **Device Sensor Fusion**: Hardware-calibrated IMU orientation (pitch, azimuth, roll, angular velocity), high-frame-rate optical capture, and acoustic microphone preservation.
2. **Multi-Domain Airspace Telemetry**: Real-time cross-referencing against live ADS-B civilian/military transponders, NOAA weather balloon soundings, and NORAD Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite trajectories.
3. **The Parallax Panel (Tri-Model AI Architecture)**: Collaborative cross-examination between three independent frontier AI engines (**Gemini**, **Claude**, and **Luna**) enforcing strict empirical falsifiability.
4. **The Dual-Lens Analytical Framework**: Simultaneous evaluation under **Lens A (Classical Fluid Dynamics & Aerospace Baseline)** and **Lens B (Theoretical Spacetime Metric Manipulation)**.

## 2. The Four Operational Pillars

Check Sky Light is architected around four distinct operational modules, accessible via the bottom tactical navigation dock:

### Pillar 1: TARGET (Optical AR Tracking HUD)
* **Real-Time Reticle HUD**: Overlays high-contrast, subpixel-stabilized azimuth (0°–360°), elevation pitch (-90° to +90°), roll, and angular tracking rates (°/sec) directly onto the live camera stream.
* **Telephoto & Optical Filtration**: Digital/optical crop-zoom controls (1x, 2x, 3x, 5x) combined with Day-HDR, Night-Starlight, and High-Contrast optical filters for acquiring high-altitude targets.
* **Acoustic Silence Tenet**: Operates completely silent by default (synthesizers and shutter sounds muted) so the microphone records clean, uncontaminated ambient acoustic signatures (Doppler shifts, infrasound, propulsion hums, or absolute silence).

### Pillar 2: RADAR (Live Airspace Deconfliction)
* **ADS-B Transponder Integration**: Live tracking of commercial airliners, private aviation, and unblocked transponders within the observer's visual radius.
* **NOAA Weather Balloon Tracking**: Live telemetry from national weather service radiosonde launches (frequency, ascent rate, altitude MSL).
* **LEO Satellite Ephemeris**: Orbital trajectory vectors for Starlink trains, the International Space Station (ISS), and prominent orbital debris.
* **Deconfliction-First Principle**: If a target correlates in azimuth, altitude, and velocity with a known transponder or satellite pass, it is immediately deconflicted as conventional before any anomalous claim can be made.

### Pillar 3: ANALYZE (Multi-Lens Forensic Intelligence Lab)
* **Digital Media & CGI Forensics**: Screens uploaded images and video frames for synthetic diffusion noise, edge matting splines, 3D camera tracking drift, and post-production keyframing.
* **Dual-Lens Analytical Engine**:
  * *Lens A (Classical Aerospace)*: Evaluates FAA Title 14 CFR § 91.209 anti-collision strobes (1.2 Hz), aerodynamic lift surfaces, combustion exhaust plumes, and Newtonian drag.
  * *Lens B (Metric Manipulation & Decoupled Physics)*: Evaluates authentic signatures of localized spacetime manipulation—transmedium travel without hydrodynamic cavitation, hypersonic vector changes without sonic booms, and positive lift without rotor wash.
* **Tri-Model Consensus**: Cross-references findings across Gemini, Claude, and Luna to prevent single-model hallucination.

### Pillar 4: REPORT (Diagnostic Dossiers & Open Field Archival)
* **Automotive Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTC)**: Assigns standardized fault codes (e.g., `DTC P0312`, `DTC P0420`, `DTC P0505`) to communicate encounter severity and category instantly.
* **Multi-Observer Triangulation**: Calculates true 3D geometric altitude (ft MSL) and slant range (mi) using simultaneous two-point witness sightline intersections.
* **Audit-Ready Export**: Generates tamper-evident, publication-grade PDF dossiers complete with sensor provenance stamps ready for submission to academic and governmental repositories (AARO, SUAPS, SCU).

### Detailed Ecosystem Breakdown & Honest Critique

#### 1. Check Sky Light (v3.6) — Score: 9.5 / 10
* **Core Strength**: Complete convergence. Fuses live AR tracking, immediate multi-source airspace deconfliction, multi-model AI forensic evaluation, and formal PDF dossiers into an accessible, zero-install mobile application.
* **Fair Limitation**: Does not operate as an all-sky automated 24/7 rooftop optical station like Sky360; requires a human operator to point and record.

#### 2. Enigma Labs (Enigma App) — Score: 5.5 / 10
* **Strengths**: High visual polish, active global social feed, intuitive post-sighting questionnaire, backing from significant venture capital.
* **Critique & Gaps**: Operates as a "black box" data silo. Sighting scores are generated by proprietary closed algorithms without published peer review. Lacks a real-time through-the-lens AR telemetry HUD, lacks multi-model epistemological consensus, does not provide raw mathematical triangulation tools, and does not export standardized scientific PDF dossiers with explicit provenance labels.

#### 3. Sky360 Network — Score: 6.6 / 10
* **Strengths**: Outstanding open-source citizen science community; continuous 24/7 sky hemisphere monitoring using dedicated fisheye and Pan-Tilt-Zoom (PTZ) optical cameras.
* **Critique & Gaps**: High financial and technical barrier to entry (requires purchasing, building, wiring, and mounting specialized rooftop camera rigs). Inflexible for mobile observers encountering an anomaly away from their home base.

#### 4. UFODAP (UFO Data Acquisition Project) — Score: 5.5 / 10
* **Strengths**: Exceptional hardware-level multi-spectral instrumentation (optical, RF, magnetic sensors) providing genuine physical measurement data.
* **Critique & Gaps**: Extremely expensive, stationary, and dated user interface. Lacks mobile accessibility and modern AI reasoning pipelines.

#### 5. Skywatcher.ai — Score: 6.8 / 10
* **Strengths**: Advanced automated computer vision tracking algorithms capable of acquiring fast-moving targets across automated optical turrets.
* **Critique & Gaps**: Proprietary enterprise/research architecture. Unavailable as a spontaneous, everyday handheld civilian diagnostic tool.

#### 6. MUFON CMS Mobile — Score: 2.7 / 10
* **Strengths**: Backed by the historical legacy and field investigator network of the Mutual UFO Network (founded 1969).
* **Critique & Gaps**: Essentially a database questionnaire form. No real-time camera integration, no live sensor telemetry capture, no instant deconfliction, and manual investigation backlogs that take months to resolve.
